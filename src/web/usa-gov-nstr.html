01 / Foundational rule

A public key is not evidence of citizenship.

It proves control of cryptographic signing authority. Identity, eligibility, jurisdiction, and legal authority still come from accountable government processes and authorized records.

02 / Four roots

No single identifier carries government power.

Every sensitive action must pass four independent checks. Cinderella protects key control, while agency proofing and workflow policy establish authority.

  1. 01

    Government authority

    The official registry confirms the agency, jurisdiction, purpose, active key, and revocation status.

  2. 02

    Cinderella key control

    A recoverable threshold approves the complete event. One device or operator cannot silently sign alone.

  3. 03

    Authoritative proofing

    The responsible agency verifies identity, citizenship, eligibility, evidence, or professional authority.

  4. 04

    Case authorization

    The gateway binds the signer to one case, role, purpose, workflow state, challenge, and expiration.

03 / System map

A banyan, not a monolith.

Each branch owns a narrow service workflow. The shared trunk supplies policy, and the roots keep signing, proofing, protected records, and audit independent.

04 / Initial catalog

One narrow workflow per service.

The first catalog spans federal, state, local, and cross-government responsibilities. It is representative, not exhaustive.

05 / Shared execution

The signed workflow

Nostr coordinates actions and receipts. Sensitive records move directly between the citizen and an encrypted government vault.

  1. 1Discover

    Find the responsible service and jurisdiction.

  2. 2Verify

    Confirm registry authority and active keys.

  3. 3Authorize

    Approve the exact action through Cinderella.

  4. 4Prove

    Complete the agency's authoritative checks.

  5. 5Protect

    Store sensitive records in the encrypted vault.

  6. 6Receipt

    Publish a minimal signed workflow event.

  7. 7Decide

    Require the correct role and workflow state.

  8. 8Close

    Deliver, retain, revoke, and audit by policy.

Private relay

Minimal coordination

Opaque case aliases, event types, signatures, timestamps, policy versions, and encrypted-object commitments.

Government vault

Protected records

Names, addresses, identifiers, evidence, forms, financial data, medical data, decisions, and deliverables.

06 / Implementation reality

The architecture can start small. Government transformation cannot be built alone.

A production system would require sustained public funding and shared ownership across agencies, lawmakers, civil-rights and privacy experts, cryptographers, security engineers, accessibility specialists, service designers, operators, auditors, and the public.
